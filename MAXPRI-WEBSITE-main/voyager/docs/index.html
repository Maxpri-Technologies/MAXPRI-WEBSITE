Product guide · Version 5

What's new in v5

Version 5 makes Voyager more useful across repeat tasks and ongoing work. Your preferences can carry forward, conversations can be tied to your Google account, and the agent is improved to plan and complete tasks more effectively.

Release highlights

More continuity. Less repeated setup.

Keep useful context between sessions, return to previous chats, and hand off multi-step browser work with clearer progress.

✦

Memory for your preferences

Save facts and preferences you choose, such as your preferred name or formatting style, and manage or clear them in the Memory panel.

◷

Chats saved with your Google account

Sign in to keep conversations associated with your account, then return to saved chats from Voyager's chat list.

↗

Smarter, more efficient agents

Voyager breaks goals into steps, reads the current page to choose relevant controls, and shows task progress as it works.

Install Voyager

Voyager is currently installed manually in Chrome.

Get the Voyager download

Open the Voyager download page and complete the subscription/download steps.

Unzip the download

Extract the downloaded ZIP file into a folder you can keep. Chrome loads the extracted folder, not the ZIP itself.

Open Chrome extensions

Navigate to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.

Load Voyager

Select Load unpacked and choose the extracted extension folder. Pin Voyager from Chrome's extensions menu for easier access.

Keep the folder in place. If you move or delete the extracted folder, Chrome may no longer be able to load the extension.

Run your first task

Voyager works in the active Chrome session. Open the website you want it to use, then describe the outcome you want rather than scripting each click.

Open the Voyager side panel

Choose the Voyager extension icon in Chrome. If prompted, sign in with Google to use account-linked chat history.

Open the relevant website

Sign in to the site yourself and navigate to the page where you want Voyager to work.

Describe one clear goal

Enter your request in the task box. For example: “Compare these three plans and summarize the monthly price and cancellation terms.”

Review and supervise

Watch the task progress and read the activity log. Check the result when Voyager finishes; approve sensitive actions when asked.

Tip: Start with a specific, low-risk task. Include the desired output and any constraints, such as which fields to compare or what not to submit.

Use memory

Memory is for information you intentionally want Voyager to remember about your preferences. It is optional; add only details you are comfortable saving.

Open Memory

In the Voyager side panel, expand Memory near the activity/log area.

Add a label and value

Enter a short label and the preference or fact to remember, then choose Save.

Review or clear saved entries

Return to the Memory panel to review saved items. Use Clear to remove memory when you no longer want Voyager to retain it.

Example
Label: Preferred date format
Value: YYYY-MM-DD
Keep sensitive data out of memory. Do not save passwords, payment details, authentication codes, or other secrets.

Save and return to chats

Sign in with Google so Voyager can associate your conversations with your Google account. Your chats are then available from the chat list in the side panel.

Sign in

Choose Sign In in the Voyager panel and complete Google's sign-in flow.

Start or continue a conversation

Enter a task in the active conversation. Use the chat list to reopen a previous chat, or select New conversation to start fresh.

Continue later

After signing in again, choose the conversation from your chat list and continue with the context there.

Account access: Google sign-in is used for account-linked features. Only grant permissions you understand and want to use.

Get your dashboard briefing

Voyager can prepare a concise dashboard report that combines your upcoming schedule with an inbox overview, so you can see what needs attention at a glance.

This week and next

Review the activities and calendar events scheduled for the current week and the following week.

Inbox overview

See a short summary of your inbox, including the five emails Voyager identifies as most important to review.

One report on your dashboard

Use the briefing to spot upcoming commitments and prioritize email without opening each item individually.

Try asking
“Prepare my dashboard briefing: summarize this week's and next week's activities, give me an inbox overview, and highlight the top five emails.”
Review before acting. The briefing is a summary to help you prioritize. Open the original calendar event or email to verify details before replying, changing plans, or taking action.

Get better results from the agent

Clear goals help Voyager plan efficiently. Describe the outcome, scope, and limits; split unrelated work into separate tasks.

A useful task prompt includes

1

The outcome

Say what you want completed: find, compare, organize, or draft.

2

The scope

Specify the pages, items, or number of results to include.

3

The boundaries

Make it explicit when Voyager should not submit, purchase, delete, or send anything.

Example
“Find 3 options under $100. Compare shipping and return policies.
Do not purchase anything.”

Safety and control

Voyager operates in your browser, where pages may contain private account information. Keep an eye on the activity log and verify important results directly on the website.

✓

Review sensitive actions

Payments, sends, deletions, and other consequential actions should be reviewed and explicitly confirmed by you.

Ⅱ

Stop when needed

Use the stop control in the side panel if the task is going in the wrong direction or you want to take over.

◎

Protect your information

Do not include passwords or other secrets in a task prompt or saved memory. Sign out of accounts on shared devices.
